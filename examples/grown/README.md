# A seed after one month (fictional)

What intent-seed's part of things might look like after a month of use by one fictional person, who writes a weekly report and a quarterly summary for their team. Everything here is invented.

The memories live in whatever memory the person's assistant already has, so they are shown here as a plain list. The decision log and the goal card are the two project files intent-seed adds.

- [`memories.md`](memories.md): what the assistant now remembers about this person and project, including one corrected entry.
- [`decision-log.md`](decision-log.md): two times the person decided against the assistant, with outcomes.
- [`goal-cards/quarterly-summary.md`](goal-cards/quarterly-summary.md): the goal card for one line of work.

Things to notice:

- Most memories are **phrase meanings**: the assistant has learned what this person's short complaints usually point at.
- One memory was **superseded** after a correction, not deleted. The old reading is still visible as history.
- The decision log has outcomes that go **both ways**: once the person was right against the assistant's advice, once the assistant's concern turned out to be justified. Neither is used to win the next argument; together they show where each judgment tends to hold up.
- After a month, routine follow-ups get a one-line restoration; only new directions get all five parts.
